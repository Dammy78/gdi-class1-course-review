# Completion process (overview)

## Concept

A learner **completes** the course when they meet the **approved minimum competency standard** on **both**:

1. **Summative theory** (supervised, closed-book; competency-based)  
2. **Summative practical** (MOD-07; competency-based)  

Attendance alone is **insufficient**.

## Eligibility themes (summary)

| Gate | Summary |
|------|---------|
| Theory summative | Required before MOD-07 in the delivery sequence |
| Practical summative | MOD-07 competency demonstration |
| Documentation | Records support completion decision (provider processes) |
| Certificate | Sample layout for application — **not** live issuance on this review site |

## Remediation

Learners who do not yet meet competency may receive remediation and reassessment per course rules. Completion is not recorded until criteria are met.

## Provider-stage items (not public)

Portal notification, live certificate issuance, retention schedules, and decision-authority roles are **provider-stage** and excluded from this site.

## Status reminder

Until NZTA **course** and applicable **provider** steps are complete, learners must **not** be told they hold NZTA-recognised course completion.
