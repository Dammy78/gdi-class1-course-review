  <h1>Practical training overview (MOD-07)</h1>
  <p style="font-size: 19px; max-width: 820px;">MOD-07 is a <strong>single continuous</strong> on-road session combining instruction and evaluation.</p>

  <div style="margin-top: 24px; padding: 20px 24px; border: 2px solid var(--color-accent-700); background: var(--color-accent-100); font-size: 16px; max-width: 900px;">
    Delivery must be conducted by personnel meeting <strong>NZTA provider requirements</strong> when the course is approved for delivery. <strong>No one is authorised to deliver based on this public site alone.</strong>
  </div>

  <h2 style="margin-top: 56px;">Session shape (one continuous session)</h2>
  <div class="phases-rail">
    <div class="phase-cell" style="background: var(--color-bg);">
      <span class="phase-n">1</span>
      <span class="phase-t">Pre-drive</span>
      <span class="phase-d">Briefing, checks, route overview at category level</span>
    </div>
    <div class="phase-cell" style="background: var(--color-surface);">
      <span class="phase-n">2</span>
      <span class="phase-t">Assisted phase</span>
      <span class="phase-d">Coaching permitted; builds toward unassisted driving</span>
    </div>
    <div class="phase-cell" style="background: var(--color-neutral-300);">
      <span class="phase-n">3</span>
      <span class="phase-t">Documented transition</span>
      <span class="phase-d"></span>
    </div>
    <div class="phase-cell" style="background: var(--color-accent-700); color: #fff;">
      <span class="phase-n">4</span>
      <span class="phase-t">Unassisted phase</span>
      <span class="phase-d">Minimum 30 minutes continuous unassisted assessed driving. Intervention for safety or compliance only.</span>
    </div>
    <div class="phase-cell" style="background: var(--color-bg);">
      <span class="phase-n">5</span>
      <span class="phase-t">Debrief</span>
      <span class="phase-d">Feedback and next steps (including supervised practice)</span>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; font-size: 15px; margin-top: 10px;">
    <span><strong>Contact time (course design):</strong> 2.5 h total for MOD-07 including pre-drive, phases, and debrief.</span>
  </div>

  <h2 style="margin-top: 56px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Competency domains (public overview)</h2>
  <p style="font-size: 16px; margin: 0 0 20px;">Twelve practical competency areas (<strong>PC-01–PC-12</strong>) cover, in summary:</p>
  <div class="pc-grid">
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-01</div><div class="pc-t">Scanning and naming hazards in time</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-02</div><div class="pc-t">Speed/position adjustments to reduce risk</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-03</div><div class="pc-t">Observation at conflicts</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-04</div><div class="pc-t">Safe gap, yield, and priority choices</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-05</div><div class="pc-t">Speed suited to conditions and limits</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-06</div><div class="pc-t">Lane position and following margin</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-07</div><div class="pc-t">Predictable interaction with other road users</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-08</div><div class="pc-t">Smooth control; continuous independent driving</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-09</div><div class="pc-t">Commentary linking see → risk → action</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-10</div><div class="pc-t">Road rules and learner conditions</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-11</div><div class="pc-t">Pre-drive identity, licence, and vehicle checks</div></div>
    <div class="pc-cell"><div class="pc-id">PC-12</div><div class="pc-t">Safety — interventions and session integrity</div></div>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size: 16px; margin: 12px 0 0;">Provider scoring sheets and route-specific maps are <strong>not</strong> published here.</p>

  <h2 style="margin-top: 56px; margin-bottom: 12px;">Relationship to restricted practical test</h2>
  <p style="font-size: 17px; margin: 0; max-width: 820px;">This session <strong>is not</strong> the restricted practical test and <strong>does not</strong> predict test outcome. Continued supervised practice remains essential.</p>
</div>
