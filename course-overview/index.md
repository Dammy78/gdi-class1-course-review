# Course overview

**Public review excerpt · course documentation version 0.1.0-review**

## Course purpose

Prepare Class 1 **learner** licence holders to progress safely toward **restricted (Class 1R)** driving by building experience, knowledge, and skills **beyond passing the Class 1R practical test**, through **facilitated theory** and **one continuous on-road practical session** with instruction and evaluation.

This course is designed for the **Graduated Driver Licensing System (GDLS) approved-course pathway**. **This course is not NZTA-approved.** After any future course and provider approval, completion may contribute to learner-period reduction only as NZTA records it.

## Intended learners

- Holders of a current **Class 1 learner licence**
- Encouraged to have **some supervised driving experience** before starting
- **Self-declaration** of readiness at enrolment

## Course components

| Component | Delivery | Modules |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Theory education | Facilitated in-person sessions | MOD-01–MOD-06 |
| Practical instruction & evaluation | One continuous on-road session | MOD-07 |
| Supervised practice between sessions | Learner and supervisor (encouraged) | Between theory modules |

**Contact time (course design):** **20.5 hours** total — **18.0 h** facilitated theory including summative theory sitting, plus **2.5 h** MOD-07 practical. This is a **course design decision**, not stated here as an NZTA national duration rule.

## Assessment approach (summary only)

| Layer | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Formative theory | Activities in MOD-01–06 and workbook |
| Summative theory | Supervised closed-book assessment after MOD-06; competency-based against mapped learning outcomes |
| Formative practical | Supported phase within MOD-07 |
| Summative practical | Independent driving phase plus competency observation |

Detailed summative instruments and provider scoring procedures are **not** on this site.

## Product separation

This regulatory course package is **separate from other GDI commercial training products**. It must not be described as already approved for delivery until NZTA course and applicable provider steps are complete.

## Progression honesty

The course **does not replace** supervised practice or guarantee restricted test pass. It **complements** supervised practice and, when approved and completed per applicable rules, may support **learner holding period reduction** policy.
