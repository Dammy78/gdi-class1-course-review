# Module 2 — Risk factors & hazard awareness

> Part of a course **under development** for NZTA approval — not NZTA recognition by itself.

## Purpose

Develop understanding of speed, impairment, distraction, environment, vulnerable road users, and developing hazards.

## Learning focus

- LO-01 and LO-02 (theory components)
- Scanning and early hazard identification concepts

## What learners do

- Scenario walk-throughs and worksheets (formative)
- Off-road scanning exercises where used by facilitator

## Practical link

Hazard types introduced here are referenced again in MOD-07 briefing and commentary.
