# Multi-location route governance (overview)

## Design principle

The course uses a **multi-location route framework**. **Course approval** submits **how routes are designed, validated, and maintained**—not a single fixed street map for all of New Zealand.

Each delivery location maintains **local routes** that satisfy the standard before use with learners.

## What a local route must support (summary)

- Opportunities to observe competency domains used in MOD-07  
- Hazard types appropriate to the local network  
- Segments suitable for **supported** orientation and **independent** continuous driving  
- Documented route identity, version, and review status at **delivery** time  

## At course approval vs at delivery

| Stage | Expectation |
|-------|-------------|
| **Course approval (this review)** | Framework, principles, competency opportunity matrix concept, empty register template |
| **Provider delivery** | Populated local route records, physical survey, activation checklist |

## Not on this public site

- Named street-level maps for a specific centre  
- Provider QMS route approval workflows  
- Personal identifiers or provider-only registers
