# Course structure & 20.5-hour delivery

## Module map

| Module | Title (summary) | Contact (example design) |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| MOD-01 | Road safety, GDLS & safe progression | 3.0 h |
| MOD-02 | Risk factors & hazard awareness | 3.0 h |
| MOD-03 | Behaviour, decisions & influences | 3.0 h |
| MOD-04 | Practical risk management (classroom) | 2.75 h |
| MOD-05 | Legal requirements & licence conditions | 2.25 h |
| MOD-06 | Readiness, reflection & summative theory | 2.0 h + 2.0 h summative |
| MOD-07 | One continuous on-road practical session | 2.5 h |

**Total contact:** **20.5 h** (18.0 h theory path including summative sitting + 2.5 h MOD-07).

Breaks between blocks are **not** counted as contact time.

## Example session sequence (course design)

| Day | Block | Example clock | Contact |
|-----|-------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | MOD-01 | 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 3.0 h |
| 2 | MOD-02 | 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 3.0 h |
| 3 | MOD-03 | 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 3.0 h |
| 4 | MOD-04 | 9:00 AM – 11:45 AM | 2.75 h |
| 5 | MOD-05 | 9:00 AM – 11:15 AM | 2.25 h |
| 6 | MOD-06 + summative theory | Morning + afternoon blocks | 4.0 h |
| 7 | MOD-07 practical | Example 8:30 AM – 11:00 AM | 2.5 h |

Calendar spacing between days may be used to embed learning; that spacing is **outside** the 20.5 h contact total.

## Theory teaching approach (summary)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Teaching** | Facilitated instruction across MOD-01–06 |
| **Formative practice** | Workbook and classroom activities — not completion evidence alone |
| **Summative assessment** | After MOD-06 — supervised closed-book; competency-based |

Facilitated, in-person delivery is the default for theory modules (not standalone distance self-study as the sole delivery mode).
