# Module 7 — Practical on-road session (learner summary)

> This session is part of a course **under development** for a future NZTA approval application. It does **not** guarantee NZTA recognition, learner-period reduction, or restricted test pass.

## Purpose

One **continuous** on-road practical instruction and evaluation session applying course learning in real traffic.

## What you demonstrate (overview)

- Early hazard detection and scanning  
- Safer speed, position, and gap choices  
- Hazard commentary during independent driving phase  
- Lawful driving on a learner licence  
- Pre-drive checks (licence, vehicle)  

Twelve practical competency areas (**PC-01–PC-12**) are used in delivery. Scoring procedures are **not** published on this site.

## Session shape (one continuous session)

1. Pre-drive (identity, licence, vehicle, briefing)  
2. **Supported** phase — coaching and orientation  
3. Documented transition  
4. **Independent** phase — minimum continuous independent driving plus commentary  
5. Debrief  

**Contact time (course design):** 2.5 h total for MOD-07 including pre-drive, phases, and debrief.

## If competency is not yet met

Remediation and reassessment may be offered per course rules. Completion is not recorded until required competencies are demonstrated.
