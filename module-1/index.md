# Module 1 — Road safety, GDLS & safe progression

> **Important:** This course is **under development** for a future NZTA course approval application. Completing review material does **not** give NZTA recognition, licence advantages, or an official certificate.

## Purpose

Introduce the Graduated Driver Licensing System (GDLS), Safe System thinking, and how this course fits the learner → restricted pathway—including the role of **supervised practice** alongside facilitated sessions.

## Learning focus

- LO-01 (introduction) and LO-09 (supervised practice planning)
- Why structured learning complements supervised hours
- Entry readiness and honest expectations

## What learners do

- Facilitated discussion on risks, responsibilities, and course pathway
- Optional short formative checks (not summative assessment)

## Links to later modules

Sets context for hazard work (MOD-02), legal conditions (MOD-05), and lawful on-road behaviour (MOD-07).
