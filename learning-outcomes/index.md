# Learning outcomes (LO-01–LO-09)

Guidance-aligned outcomes mapped to modules and assessment pathways. Summative **instruments** are not published here.

| LO | Outcome (summary) | Primary modules | Theory / practical |
|----|-------------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| **LO-01** | Recognise key risks and hazards relevant to novice driving | MOD-01, MOD-02 | Theory |
| **LO-02** | Detect hazards through effective scanning; identify risks early | MOD-02, MOD-04, MOD-07 | Both |
| **LO-03** | Make safer driving decisions (speed, gaps, following distance, positioning) | MOD-03, MOD-07 | Both |
| **LO-04** | Demonstrate safe behaviours and hazard response in practical contexts | MOD-04, MOD-07 | Both |
| **LO-05** | Explain and apply learner licence conditions and road rules compliance | MOD-01, MOD-05, MOD-07 | Both |
| **LO-06** | Apply risk-reduction strategies (margins, checks, journey planning) | MOD-04, MOD-07 | Both |
| **LO-07** | Describe how personal, social, and environmental factors influence driving risk | MOD-03 | Theory |
| **LO-08** | Self-reflect on experience and personal risk factors | MOD-06 | Theory (formative emphasis) |
| **LO-09** | Plan continued supervised practice after the course | MOD-01, MOD-06 | Theory (formative emphasis) |

## Summative theory gate (concept)

Summative theory focuses on mapped outcomes including LO-01, LO-03, LO-05, LO-06, and LO-07 (and theory components of hazard awareness). Delivery is **supervised closed-book**. Completion is based on **demonstrated competency** against mapped outcomes, not a legacy percentage pass rule.

LO-08 and LO-09 are primarily supported through MOD-06 formative activities and completion discussion.
