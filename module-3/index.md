# Module 3 — Behaviour, decisions & influences

## Purpose

Explore how drivers perceive risk, make decisions at conflict points, and how social and emotional factors affect choices.

## Learning focus

- LO-03 and LO-07
- Safer decisions on speed, gaps, and following distance

## What learners do

- Scenario tables and facilitated reflection (formative)
- Discussion of peer pressure, fatigue, and common novice errors

## Practical link

Decision-making frameworks support MOD-07 observation domains. Provider scoring detail is not published on this site.
