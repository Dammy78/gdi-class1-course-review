  <h2>Summative theory gate (concept)</h2>
  <p>Summative theory focuses on mapped outcomes including LO-01, LO-03, LO-05, LO-06, and LO-07 (and theory components of hazard awareness). Delivery is <strong>supervised closed-book</strong>. Completion is based on <strong>demonstrated competency</strong> against mapped outcomes, not a legacy percentage pass rule.</p>
  <p>LO-08 and LO-09 are primarily supported through MOD-06 formative activities and completion discussion.</p>
